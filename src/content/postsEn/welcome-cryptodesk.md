Welcome to the **English edition of CryptoDesk** — sober, well-documented analysis of
cryptocurrencies and financial markets, published **after the US market close**.

The French edition focuses on daily analysis from a European perspective. This English
edition takes the same approach but is written for the US market: it covers the day's
moves, regulation news, DeFi developments and macro signals — with the freshest
information from the US session.

## What you'll find here

- **Daily market analysis** — published around 7 PM ET, after the close
- **Regulation watch** — CLARITY Act, SEC, CFTC, and their market impact
- **Polymarket signals** — prediction-market probabilities on Bitcoin and macro events
- **DeFi & protocols** — projects generating real revenue, with accumulation levels

## Not investment advice

Everything here is informational context to help you decide for yourself.
Always do your own research.
